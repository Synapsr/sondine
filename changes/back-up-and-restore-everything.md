## Back up all of Sondine in one file, and get it all back at once

Settings ▸ Data saves your conversations, transcripts, voices, people, journal, context and settings to one file, protected by a passphrase unless you choose otherwise. After a reinstall or on another Mac, choose « Restaurer une sauvegarde… » and everything comes back. The data it replaces is kept aside, and listening stays paused until you resume it.
