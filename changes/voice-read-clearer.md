## Reading your voice, clearer

In the first run, « Learn my voice » is a card with your first name and whether Sondine knows your voice; « Recognize me » opens the read in a wide dialog, which closes by itself once your voice is learned, a check on the card saying so. While you read, the progress shows as a percentage, with bars that follow your voice, and nothing on the page moves when it starts. Once done, a card on the moving sky says Sondine knows your voice.
