## Try the Sondine Cloud for free, then subscribe from Sondine

Choosing Sondine Cloud now shows how full the current wave is and offers 60 free minutes in one click, with no card and no account. In Settings ▸ Transcription you can then subscribe to the Founder plan (19 € a month for 40 hours), add 10 hours for 10 €, and manage your card, invoices or cancellation: each opens Stripe's secure page in your browser, and Sondine updates by itself once you have paid. Transcriptions that waited for hours start again on their own.
