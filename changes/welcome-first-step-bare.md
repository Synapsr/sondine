## A barer welcome

The welcome's screens show only their question: no more progress bar above them. On the first, « Restore a backup… » moves to the top corner, in light text, and the card you chose is marked in grey, not black. Moving between steps no longer jumps up or down, and the next step comes in from the side you go toward. Granting a permission no longer moves the text below it.
