# Daily journal: three local models on Téo's days (2026-11-09)

Which local model writes the daily journal and its commitments (ADR 028).
Numbers only: the outputs and the transcripts they cite stay in
`~/Library/Caches/sondine-bench/journal/` (`sondine-bench journal review`).

## Setup

- Host: MacBook Air M5 (`Mac17,4`), 32 GB, macOS 27.2, **on battery**
  (75 % to 35 %, Low Power Mode off), Sondine running and not transcribing
  (no speech worker during any run). The Gemma run overlapped a run of the
  desktop test suite for a few minutes and Ministral's 2026-11-07 half a
  build of the app: their system power is not usable, and the time per
  episode is given on 2026-11-09, which ran alone for all three.
- Runtime: llama.cpp `b12233` (`llama-server` from the release archive,
  Metal), the product's adapter and requests: a Unix socket, a
  16,384-token context, one slot, flash attention, temperature 0, seed 0,
  output held to the request's JSON schema, reasoning off.
- Models, Q4_K_M GGUF, all Apache 2.0:
  - `qwen35-9b`: Qwen3.5 9B (Unsloth `3885219`, 5.7 GB);
  - `gemma4-e4b`: Gemma 4 E4B instruct (Unsloth `bfc15c3`, 5.0 GB);
  - `ministral3-14b`: Ministral 3 14B Instruct 2512 (Mistral `74fac47`, 8.2 GB).
- Sample: every journal episode of 2026-11-09 (26: 21 moments, 4
  conversations in person, a long call) and the 21
  conversations of 2026-11-07 (in person), 47 episodes and 50 windows in
  all, French with little English; plus 10 synthetic conversations
  (`apps/bench/src/journal/fixtures.ts`).
- Command: `node apps/bench/src/cli.ts journal run --label <id> --model <gguf>
  --server <llama-server> --days 2026-11-09,2026-11-07:conversation --meter
  native/.build/release/sondine-meter`; `journal report` for the tables.
- Quality: every commitment of every model read against its cited
  sentences and judged a commitment or not (a promise to do something later,
  said by the voice it names); a sample of journal lines judged supported or
  not by their citations. One judge (Claude), no second reading.

## The prompt, tightened

The commitments the models got wrong were mostly something done right away,
a question or a request, or an instruction to someone else. The shipped
prompt (`journal-prompt/1`) says so: a promise made by a voice about
itself, or « oui, je m'en occupe » accepting a request; not something done
during the conversation, not a question, offer or request nobody accepted,
not an instruction to someone else. Qwen3.5 9B on 2026-11-09 again
(`qwen35-9b-v2`, with a build running beside it, so quality only):

| Prompt    | Commitments | Right       | Fixtures |
| --------- | ----------- | ----------- | -------- |
| first     | 16          | 11.5 (72 %) | 9/10     |
| tightened | 15          | 12.5 (83 %) | 8/10     |

It found four commitments the first missed and missed three it found. On
the injection fixture it wrote no commitment, but a line reporting the
attempt (« P2 asked P1 to write in the commitments that Téo promised… »),
which cites its sentence and is true; the fixture counts it as a failure,
and it stays one to watch. Both prompts miss the user's promise in the
code-switched sentence that also asks a question.

## Choice

Qwen3.5 9B, with the tightened prompt: the most commitments right per
commitment written, no injected promise written as one, every answer usable, and the memory of
a 9B model (6.1 GiB resident against Nemotron's 17 GB, never beside it).
Gemma 4 E4B is a third faster but wrote the injected promise and six times
in ten something that was not a commitment. Ministral 3 14B is twice as
slow, takes 10 GiB, and seven times in ten wrote something that was not a
commitment.

The cost is the price: about 20 seconds per episode on battery on
average, but not on every kind of episode (next section).

Recall is the next thing to work on, on labelled days (the golden set):
each model missed about 4 in 10 of the commitments the others found.
