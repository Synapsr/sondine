# A tap per app playing sound — measurements (ADR 026)

Téo's Mac: MacBook Air M5, macOS 27.2 (26B123), on AC, 2026-11-09 evening.
Two signed builds on the same data, one after the other:

- **V0**: `0bcdb6a` (main before this change): `system:other` always on.
- **V2**: this change: a tap per app playing, `system:other`
  only while something in its scope plays.

Every run kept the microphone paused (Téo had paused Sondine; nothing of the
room was recorded) and the Mac's output muted, and ran only while nobody had
touched the Mac for 10 minutes; any input stopped the run and put Sondine
back as Téo left it. What the runs recorded was forgotten afterwards, as
« Oublier » does (episodes, regions, audio bytes). `sondine-meter` through
`budget run`, 1 min settle, 10 min measured; the bench player apps are
`afplay` copies in ad-hoc app bundles (`bench.sondine.player-a`, `-b`) playing
`say` speech (French, then English, each about 9 min, looped).

## Budget

The microphone is paused in every row, so these numbers isolate system
audio. coreaudiod's mean moved between 1.0 % and 2.0 % with nothing of
Sondine's changing (S6, V0 S0, V2 S0): other apps' IO, about a point of
noise.

| Run                                | Taps           | `sondine-audio` CPU | `core` CPU | Sondine total CPU | Sondine energy | `sondine-audio` footprint | coreaudiod CPU |
| ---------------------------------- | -------------- | ------------------: | ---------: | ----------------: | -------------: | ------------------------: | -------------: |
| S6 paused (baseline)               | none           |             0.036 % |    0.077 % |           0.138 % |        0.35 mW |                  10.0 MiB |         1.98 % |
| S0 silence, V0                     | `system:other` |             0.170 % |    0.191 % |           0.410 % |        1.01 mW |                  28.6 MiB |         1.96 % |
| S0 silence, V2 (9 min)             | none           |             0.025 % |    0.064 % |           0.121 % |        0.46 mW |                  10.4 MiB |         1.02 % |
| S3e one app speaking, V0           | `system:other` |             1.249 % |    0.296 % |           1.606 % |        2.77 mW |                  27.0 MiB |        11.15 % |
| S5e two apps speaking, V0          | `system:other` |             1.373 % |    0.297 % |           1.733 % |        2.49 mW |                  26.9 MiB |        15.38 % |
| S3e one app speaking, V2           | its app's tap  |             1.235 % |    0.271 % |           1.561 % |        2.84 mW |                  29.8 MiB |        10.88 % |
| S5e two apps speaking, V2 (10 min) | two app taps   |             2.143 % |    0.414 % |           2.649 % |        5.27 mW |                  51.8 MiB |        11.55 % |

- **Silence.** The idle `system:other` tap costs coreaudiod nothing
  measurable on macOS 27.2 (V0 S0 against S6: −0.01 point); it costs Sondine
  0.27 point of CPU and 0.66 mW (the helper drains, resamples and gates
  zeros, core imports a commit every 2 s, the spool writes 115 MB/h of
  them). V2 runs no tap in silence: Sondine at the paused level. The +1.7
  points M0 measured on 27.0.1 (architecture §5) came with an unpaused
  microphone; whether the microphone's processing in coreaudiod is the cost
  is not measured here (it would record the room).
- **Playing.** coreaudiod's +9 to +13 points with one or two players include
  the playback itself (mixing and the speakers' processing, muted or not);
  V0 and V2 differ only in the taps, under the same playback.
- **One app.** Its own tap costs what `system:other` did: Sondine 1.56 %
  against 1.61 %, coreaudiod 10.9 % against 11.2 %.
- **Two apps at once.** Two taps cost coreaudiod no more than one global tap
  (11.5 % against 15.4 %, within its noise and its playback's), and Sondine
  0.92 point of CPU and 2.8 mW more: a second resampler, gate and VAD on a
  second stream of speech. The helper's footprint rose to 51.8 MiB, above
  the 48 MiB starting cap: each track ran its own ONNX session of the VAD.
  The change now shares one model between tracks (each keeps its own state; a
  test checks they give the same probabilities as separate models), which
  saves about 8 MiB per track beyond the first (a probe: four separate
  models 46.6 MiB, one shared 23.3 MiB). Not measured again on the Mac: Téo
  came back. Each app speaking at once still costs the helper about 14 MiB
  (rings, resampler, spool and VAD buffers).

## Real hardware, two apps at once

V2 on Téo's Mac: player A plays a 300 Hz tone for 30 s; 10 s in, player B
starts a 500 Hz tone for 30 s. After Sondine quit, each track run's spool
PCM is read back and each tone's energy measured per 100 ms window
(Goertzel), as the fake-HAL fixtures do:

| Source           | `app:bench.sondine.player-a` | `app:bench.sondine.player-b` | `system:other` |
| ---------------- | ---------------------------: | ---------------------------: | -------------: |
| player A, 300 Hz |                       30.1 s |                        0.0 s |         no run |
| player B, 500 Hz |                        0.0 s |                       30.0 s |         no run |

Each app kept its own sound for its whole playing time, with nothing lost at
its start within the 100 ms windows, and `system:other` never started. The
first run of this test gave both tones to `system:other`: the helper, under
App Sandbox, cannot read app bundles outside `/Applications`, so the players
(in a temporary folder) had no app. Now an app's main executable outside
`/Applications` takes the bundle id Core Audio reports for it.

## Co-occurrence probe

`IsRunningOutput` of every process object, every 5 s, 18:10 to 20:10
(1 432 samples). While Téo worked (the first 44 min): output running in 77 %
of the samples, two apps or more in 40 %; Klack (keyboard sounds) 64 %, Hex
41 %, T3 Code 11 %, Yab 7 %. While he was away (76 min): output in 7 % of
the samples, T3 Code's sounds mostly.
