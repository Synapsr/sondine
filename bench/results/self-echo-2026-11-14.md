# Sondine's own echo — measurements (ADR 035)

Téo's Mac (MacBook Air M5, macOS 27.2), 2026-11-14, worktree build, no
audio recorded: synthetic signals made in the bench and the tests only.

## The detector, `envelope-echo/1`

`pnpm bench self-echo synthetic` (40 seeds per case, each a new playback,
room and voice; `packages/engines/src/self-echo/synthetic.ts`):

| case              | must be      | right | wrong (seed:verdict)                  |
| ----------------- | ------------ | ----: | ------------------------------------- |
| echo              | echo         | 40/40 | —                                     |
| quiet echo        | echo         | 40/40 | —                                     |
| noisy room        | echo         | 35/40 | 2, 15, 20, 28, 33: near-end           |
| short echo region | echo         | 39/40 | 25:no-reference                       |
| lag change        | echo         | 38/40 | 4:near-end 19:near-end                |
| airplay delay     | echo         | 39/40 | 14:near-end                           |
| double-talk       | near-end     | 40/40 | —                                     |
| soft double-talk  | near-end     | 40/40 | —                                     |
| short reply       | near-end     | 35/40 | 3:echo 9:echo 17:echo 24:echo 27:echo |
| headphones        | near-end     | 40/40 | —                                     |
| no reference      | no-reference | 40/40 | —                                     |

- Echo is the playback at −12, −26 and −18 dB (noise at −45 dBFS) heard
  40 to 280 ms late with half a second of reverberation; « lag change »
  jumps from 60 to 300 ms mid-region; « airplay delay » is 1.8 s.
- Double-talk is the user near the microphone for 10 s over the echo, 14 dB
  and 4 dB louder than it; « short reply » is 0.6 s said 14 dB over it in a
  0.9 s region.
- The wrong verdicts: a region with nothing above the noise floor (taken as
  no reference, left out as before); in the noisy room, stretches where no
  lag stands out of the noise, so nothing says the playback explains them
  (kept: when unsure, the user's words stay); near-end speech found where
  the lag jumps, and once at AirPlay's delay (kept: words said in those frames reach the
  transcript); short replies lost under the echo (left out, as before this
  change).
- CPU: 750 ms per hour of speech judged (the detector, 5 s of context each
  side included). Reading the region's audio and building its envelope
  come on top (ADR 032: building envelopes and detecting took about 0.47 s
  per hour of audio).

## The helper's reference

`swift test -c release --filter theReferenceIsCheap`: 60 s of a 48 kHz
tone drained every 256 ms through `SelfPlaybackReference` (resampling to
16 kHz, 10 ms envelope, events):

| build   | CPU per minute of playback | events per minute |
| ------- | -------------------------: | ----------------: |
| release |                     9.6 ms |                58 |
| debug   |                    29.6 ms |                58 |

9.6 ms a minute is 0.016 % of a core while Sondine plays. Nothing runs
while it does not: the tap starts when Sondine's own output runs and stops
2 s after it stops (Chromium keeps its output open about 31 s after a
sound, ADR 004, so the tap can run that long after the last one).

Not measured here: the tap itself on the real HAL (a worktree build has no
system-audio grant). ADR 026 measured an app tap's cost to coreaudiod as
within its noise of the playback's own, and its IOProc at 85 ms buffers is
about 12 wakeups a second; the reference adds one such tap and one 10 s
ring (1.9 MB at 48 kHz) while Sondine plays and the microphone listens.
Core keeps at most 15 minutes of frames in memory (360 KB).
